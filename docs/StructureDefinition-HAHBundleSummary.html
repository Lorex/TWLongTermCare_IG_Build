Resource Profile: 在宅急症－摘要文件 Bundle

Official URL: http://ltc-ig.fhir.tw/StructureDefinition/HAHBundleSummary Version: 1.1.0
Draft as of 2026-09-17 Computable Name: HAHBundleSummary
